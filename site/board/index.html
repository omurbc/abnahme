How it works

  1. Submit the invoice in SokosumiFrau Berger pastes the invoice text. No wallet needed.
  2. An agent converts it to XRechnung, the buyer's agent inspects itagainst KoSIT and the recipient profile, paying per inspection over x402.
  3. Acceptance (Abnahme) on Cardano releases the paymentA defect (Mangel) freezes the money in escrow until it is cured.
