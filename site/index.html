The problem

From 1 Jan 2027, German businesses above €800,000 turnover must send e-invoices (XRechnung / EN 16931).

A general contractor rejects an invoice that misses its own fields, and the 21-day payment clock restarts. Today's converters grade their own work.

How it works

The money waits until the invoice passes.

  1. Step 1

    Locked.

    The converter's fee is locked in a Masumi escrow on Cardano. The acceptance test (KoSIT validator 1.6.3 plus the recipient's rules) is pinned before money moves.

    Money lockedfac4e80e…22:56:46(transaction on Cardanoscan, opens in a new tab)

  2. Step 2

    Checked.

    An inspector agent, paid per check over x402, finds a defect. A defect notice (Mängelrüge, §640(2) BGB) goes on chain and the money stays frozen while the converter cures it.

    Defect noticed61baca2…22:59:40(transaction on Cardanoscan, opens in a new tab)

  3. Step 3

    Paid.

    The cured invoice passes and is accepted (Abnahme, §640(1) BGB). Only then is the converter paid (§641 BGB).
