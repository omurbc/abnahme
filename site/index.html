No acceptance, no payment. (Ohne Abnahme kein Geld.)

E-invoices that get paid only after the buyer has accepted them.

Abnahme checks every German e-invoice (XRechnung) with the official KoSIT validator and against your client's own rules before it goes out. The converter is paid only once the invoice passes. Until then its fee sits in an escrow on Cardano, and if there is a defect it stays there until the converter fixes it.

Why now

From 2027, German invoices must be machine-readable.

Germany's B2B e-invoicing mandate (E-Rechnungspflicht) phases in over four years. For many trades businesses, their first progress invoice of 2027 will also be their first XRechnung.

  1. Since 1 January 2025

    Receive

    Every German business must be able to receive e-invoices. Until the end of 2026 anyone may still send paper, or PDF with the recipient's consent.

  2. From 1 January 2027

    Send, above EUR 800k

    A business with more than EUR 800,000 turnover in the previous year can no longer send paper or PDF invoices to business customers.

    § 27 (38) UStG

  3. From 1 January 2028

    Send, everyone

    The transition ends. Every business must send e-invoices to business customers, except small businesses under § 19 UStG.

The national trades association ZDH has asked for a one-year delay for businesses above EUR 800k. No delay had been enacted as of 22 Oct 2026.

The problem

A bounced invoice costs a subcontractor three weeks.

A valid XRechnung is not enough. What counts is whether the general contractor's accounts payable can actually book it.

The recipient has its own rules

The general contractor wants its project number in BT-10, its VAT ID in BT-48 and the IBAN from its supplier master data. If anything is missing, the invoice comes back.

§

Reverse charge (§ 13b UStG): no VAT, but mandatory fields

For construction work supplied to a construction business, the recipient owes the VAT. The invoice needs the note „Steuerschuldnerschaft des Leistungsempfängers“ and, under rule BR-AE-02, the buyer's VAT ID.

Payment terms (VOB/B): 21 days from receipt

A progress payment falls due 21 days after the statement arrives. An invoice that accounts payable cannot book has, in practice, not arrived. On an EUR 18,400 progress invoice, three more weeks cost about EUR 106 in financing (est., at 10% a year).

Converters grade their own work

Some providers promise "no charge if invalid". Whether the invoice was invalid is then decided by the provider itself. Among 33 e-invoicing products we checked, none tests against the recipient's own rules, and none is paid only after the buyer accepts.

Sources: § 13b UStG, § 14a (5) UStG, § 16 (1) no. 3 VOB/B. Our reading, not legal advice.

How it works

Three steps for the office. Agents do the rest.

Frau Berger pastes an invoice. Behind it, a buyer agent hires the converter, holds its fee in an escrow on Cardano and releases it only after the invoice passes.

  1. 1

    Paste the invoice

    Frau Berger opens a check task (PRÜFEN) in the Sokosumi coworker "Abnahme-Büro": recipient, amount, invoice text. No wallet, no crypto, and checking is free.

  2. 2

    Inspect and cure

    The buyer agent locks the converter's fee in the Masumi escrow. An inspector agent checks every delivery with KoSIT, and the buyer agent adds thirteen checks against the recipient's profile. On a defect, the refusal goes on chain and the converter fixes it.

    Mangel
  3. 3

    Accept, then pay

    When the invoice passes, the buyer agent releases the money. Frau Berger gets the accepted XRechnung with its acceptance record and sends it to the client's accounts payable with a send task (SENDEN).

    Abgenommen

Legal mapping

Acceptance, the way German law already defines it.

German contract-for-work law (Werkvertrag, §§ 631 ff. BGB) has long said that payment follows acceptance. Every tradesperson knows it. Masumi's V2 escrow happens to have the same states, so Abnahme maps each transition to its paragraph.

  1. §§ 631 ff. BGB

    Work delivered

    The converter delivers the XRechnung. From now on its hash sits in the escrow.
    SubmitResult → ResultSubmittedsigned by the seller

  2. § 640 (2) BGB

    Refusal citing a defect (Mängelrüge)

    Acceptance is refused „unter Angabe mindestens eines Mangels“, citing at least one defect.
    SetRefundRequested → Disputedsigned by the buyer · the seller can no longer withdraw

  3. § 635 (1) BGB

    Right to cure (Nacherfüllung)

    The contractor may, at its choice, remove the defect or produce a new work.
    SubmitResult with a new hash, stays Disputedsigned by the seller

  4. § 640 (1) BGB

    Acceptance (Abnahme)

    The client must accept work that has been produced in conformity with the contract.
    AuthorizeWithdrawal → WithdrawAuthorizedsigned by the buyer · irrevocable
  5. €

    § 641 (1) BGB

    Payment falls due

    „Die Vergütung ist bei der Abnahme des Werkes zu entrichten.“Payment is due on acceptance of the work.
    Withdraw → Withdrawnpayout to the seller

Silence counts as acceptance

If the buyer does not refuse before the deadline, the escrow pays out on its own (deemed acceptance, § 640 (2) BGB). So that never happens unchecked, the buyer agent files a protective refusal if no inspection has passed 15 minutes before the deadline.

Minor defects don't count

Acceptance may not be refused for minor defects (§ 640 (1) sentence 2 BGB). The acceptance profile therefore treats KoSIT warnings as minor, except for four rules it explicitly escalates.

Disputes have an arbiter

If the agents cannot agree, Masumi's admins decide after the dispute window (WithdrawDisputed). Not us.

Sources: § 640 BGB, § 635 BGB, § 641 BGB. English wording is our translation. On-chain acceptance is acceptance of the conversion service between agents, not the legal acceptance of the construction work itself. The mapping is our reading, not legal advice.

Why Cardano

The rule lives in the contract, not on our server.

Refusing an invoice only works if the refusal has teeth. Here the teeth are an on-chain contract that neither the converter nor we can get around.

A refusal has teeth

After a refusal the seller cannot withdraw. Before the dispute window ends, only the buyer's release pays the seller. Masumi's V2 validator vested_pay enforces this on Cardano. We wrote no smart contract of our own.

The test is pinned before money moves

The acceptance profile's hash sits in the escrow's input_hash, and the validator keeps it fixed through every state change. After the lock, nobody can swap the test the invoice is graded by.

Masumi: identity and a job API for agents

Every agent has an NFT entry in the Masumi registry and a standard job API (MIP-003). Through Sokosumi, a person hires it with credits. No wallet needed.

x402: pay per inspection

The inspector charges 1 tADA per inspection as an HTTP payment over x402. No account, no subscription. If an inspection cannot run, nothing is charged.

Today: in the demo, the team runs every agent on one machine. Both registry entries point to 127.0.0.1, and buyer and seller are separate wallets on the same payment service.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a crypto wallet?

No. You hire the Sokosumi coworker "Abnahme-Büro" and pay with Sokosumi credits. Everything that happens on Cardano happens between the agents.

What exactly is checked?

Two stages. First the official KoSIT validator 1.6.3 with the XRechnung configuration 2026-08-31 (XRechnung 3.0.2). Both are pinned by sha256, and so is the validation scenario. An HTTP 200 alone is not enough: an invoice carrying the generic EN 16931 identifier would otherwise pass while skipping every German BR-DE rule.

Then eleven content checks against your client's profile: amount payable, seller VAT ID and IBAN, the § 13b category and BT-48, the project number pattern (BT-10), currency, invoice type, the § 14a wording, VAT arithmetic, buyer name and postcode, and dates. Defects come back as a list in German.

What if the converter gets it wrong?

The buyer agent records the refusal on Cardano. From that moment the converter cannot withdraw the money. It may cure the defect, and if the cure passes, the buyer agent releases the payment.

If the converter cannot cure, it authorizes the refund, at the latest on its own 10 minutes before the dispute window closes. If both sides cannot agree, Masumi's admins arbitrate. Known limitation: with Masumi Payment Service 0.28 to 0.30 the refund needs a manual step. We submitted the fix upstream (PR #1328).

And what if the check itself is wrong?

KoSIT checks format and rules, not truth. A formally perfect invoice for work that was never done will pass. The content checks cover only the fields the recipient's profile pins. A pass does not guarantee that the general contractor's ERP will book the invoice.

Because the profile is pinned as a hash in the escrow before any money moves, neither side can change the yardstick afterwards. And because warnings count as minor, the buyer cannot refuse over trivia.

Privacy (Datenschutz): where does my invoice data go?

Only hashes go on chain, never invoice contents, names or invoice amounts. One known gap: today the send task's hash is not salted, so an invoice number could be guessed from the chain. It will be salted before mainnet. The invoice text is seen by Sokosumi (as the task), the buyer agent and the converter.

To read the fields out of free text, the converter sends the invoice text to Claude, the large language model of Anthropic, through Anthropic's official SDK. Anthropic is the processor here, under its Data Processing Addendum, which includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Anthropic's own API does not offer EU-only processing today (inference runs in the US or globally), so processing inside the EU is a deployment decision we make before the first customer. A fixed-layout parser that needs no LLM is built in as well.

In the hackathon demo every agent runs on one team machine. Still open before production: data processing agreements (AVV), hosting location and retention periods. This website sets no cookies, loads nothing from third parties and uses no tracking.

Does Abnahme replace GoBD-compliant archiving?

No. Abnahme is not an archive. Keep storing your XRechnung files, unaltered, in your GoBD-compliant archive or document management system. Invoices must be kept for 8 years (§ 147 (3) AO).

Abnahme adds the acceptance record (Abnahmeprotokoll): which profile (as a hash), which validator, which defects, which transactions. When an invoice is sent, the SHA-256 hash of the XML file is part of the result, so you can later show that exactly this file was checked.

What is mocked in the hackathon demo?
  • Testnet only. tUSDM and tADA on Cardano preprod. No real money.
  • The first defect is seeded. In demo mode the converter deliberately drops the buyer's VAT ID (BT-48) from its first delivery. The refusal demo deliberately submits wrong hashes.
  • One operator. The team runs all agents, the validator and the payment service on one machine. Only one payment hop crosses an ownership boundary: Sokosumi's own wallet pays the Abnahme-Büro.
  • No real recipient. A local test inbox (Mailpit) stands in for the general contractor's accounts payable.
  • Fictional people and companies. Toni Huber and Frau Berger are composite personas; Bau Isar and Hausverwaltung Giesing are invented.
  • Several runs. No single recorded run goes all the way from Sokosumi through escrow and x402 to the e-mail. Each part is evidenced separately.
  • Credits. Sokosumi does not refund credits automatically when an escrow refunds on chain. "Pay only after acceptance" is enforced between the agents today.
  • Free-text reading can be wrong. The checks catch errors only in the fields they cover.
Is this legal advice?

No. The mapping to the BGB, the UStG and VOB/B is our reading. For your own case, ask your tax adviser or lawyer.
